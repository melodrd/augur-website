# Ported content

Pages carried over from the old augur.net so its URLs keep working after the domain moves to this site (owner decision, 30 October 2026). The source is `AugurProject/augur-reboot-website` at `ba18725`, the revision www.augur.net served on 30 October 2026. The pages use this site's layout, tokens and type; they do not copy the old look.

The pages are reachable by URL and through links that already existed. They are not in the header menus, on the homepage or on the Learn index (owner decision, 30 October 2026); `/sitemap-index.xml` lists them like every other page.

## Collections and rendering

- [content.config.ts](../../src/content.config.ts) defines `blog` (one folder per post; `featuredImage` is an `image()` used as the cover on the index and the post page, and post bodies don't repeat it; `topic` is `progress-report`, `moon-fork` or `research`), `whitepaperV2` (no frontmatter) and `forkGuides` (the old Learn frontmatter: `order`, `label`, `status`, `presentation` and so on). `learn` is unchanged.
- [blog.ts](../../src/data/blog.ts) orders posts newest first and formats dates in UTC for the index, the post pages and the feed. It also names each topic, estimates reading time from the MDX source at 230 words a minute, builds each post's reading queue (the next and earlier post in the same topic, then the rest of that topic nearest in time, then other topics) and renders a post as Markdown for "Copy for AI". [whitepapers.ts](../../src/data/whitepapers.ts) holds the edition facts (release `v2.0.16`, source revision `69accf6`), the section order and each section's title, taken from its `#` heading ("References" has none).
- [astro.config.mjs](../../astro.config.mjs) sets the processors with `unified()` from `@astrojs/markdown-remark`. `.md` (the whitepaper and the Learn guides) gets `remark-math`, `rehype-katex` and the whitepaper link rewrite. `.mdx` (posts and fork guides) gets no math, so `$` in prose stays text.
- [src/markdown](../../src/markdown/): `rehype-whitepaper-links` turns `file.md#x` links into `/whitepapers/augur-v2/file/#x`; `rehype-callouts` turns a post paragraph that starts with an emoji (👉) into a `.callout`; `rehype-heading-emoji` drops the emoji that open post headings; `rehype-ported-content` adds ↗ to off-site links and wraps tables in a focusable scroll box, in the three ported collections only. Links to `augur.net` or `www.augur.net` count as internal. In blog posts only, `rehype-tweets` turns a link to an X post that stands on its own line into X's embed markup (`blockquote.twitter-tweet` with `data-dnt`, `data-cards="hidden"` so the post's photo or link preview stays out and the embed stays close to the text's height, and `data-align="center"`; the post page loads X's `widgets.js` when one nears the screen, and without it the blockquote stays a link; see [open decisions](../records/open-decisions.md#analytics-and-headers)), and `rehype-post-text` marks an italic-only paragraph as a standfirst (before the first section) or sign-off (after it) and lifts a notice's bold opening ("MIGRATION CLOSED") into a label. `rehype-post-images` gives each local image in a post the widest it should show (its own width, or less where it would be taller than 760px), and the post page centres it at up to that width within the column, so small screenshots are never enlarged, portrait diagrams fit about a screen and the size is known before the image loads.
- `katex` is pinned to `^0.16`, the version `rehype-katex` renders with; the 0.18 stylesheet renames KaTeX's classes and breaks the layout. Only the whitepaper route imports its CSS.
- Shared components in [src/components/content](../../src/components/content/): `Prose` (reading styles for all ported pages), `ProseCard` (framed note used inside MDX), `ArticleHeader`, `SectionRail` (document pages and headings), `PagerNav` (previous and next) and `LegalContacts`. Images go through `astro:assets`.
- Blog components in [src/components/blog](../../src/components/blog/): `PostCard` (one post as a card, or the newest post as the featured card; it carries the topic the index filters on), `PostContents` (the post's "On this page" list of its sections, h2s only: a rail list from 1024px up and a sticky "Contents" disclosure below it, whose list opens as a panel over the text and closes when a section is chosen, on Escape or on a press or focus outside it; plain anchor links, with a small script that marks the section being read), `PostShare` (Copy for AI, Copy link and Share on X: three icon buttons with tooltips beside the byline, and a labelled "Share this post" row after the text; the copy buttons appear only when the script can copy, and "Copy for AI" copies `postMarkdown()` from blog.ts, embedded once per page as JSON) and `BlogMotion` (an entrance on arrival, once per tab session like the site's other entrances in [seen.ts](../../src/motion/seen.ts), and reveals for cards that scroll in, through `playOnce`; none under reduced motion or in print).
- The fork migration record (`presentation: historical-record`) keeps the old archive header: "Archive · Migration closed", "Moon Fork migration is closed".

## Differences from the old pages

The words are the old pages' words except where listed here.

- **Fork-risk monitor removed** (owner decision; the monitor and `/data/fork-risk.json` are dropped, and migration closed on 3 August 2026). In `index.mdx`: the learning outcome "read the site's fork monitor as a measurement of protocol state, not a probability forecast; and" (the bullet before it now ends "; and") and the section "What the monitor means". In `disputes-and-bonds.mdx`: "and how the fork monitor measures progress" from the description, and the section "How to read fork progress". In `what-to-do.mdx`: the section "Use the monitor as a signal" with its table, and the bullet "Do not treat a monitoring label as financial advice or as a guarantee that a fork will occur." Mentions of ForkWatch (v3.augur.net), a separate site, are kept.
- **Fork guide titles**: each guide's `# Title` line moved out of the body; the page header shows the same title as the page's `h1`.
- **Blog layout**: `/blog/` has a short header, a row of topic filters (wrapping onto a second line on phones), the newest post as a featured card (with its description) and every other post in a three-column grid of faintly outlined cards (cover, title, then date and reading time) below it, with no heading of its own. The grid shows nine cards a page with page links under it (← 1 2 →). Without JavaScript the filters and page links are hidden and every post shows; with them, the grid shows one topic and one page at a time, `?topic=` and `?page=` keep both in the address, and the back button steps back through pages. Switching topic reflows the grid through a view transition. Post pages draw a green reading-progress line along the top of the window, run a side rail ("On this page", sections only) beside the title and the text, put share actions beside the byline and after the text, set the header, the cover from frontmatter and the text in one 680px column of 18px text (centred below 1024px) with a green square beside each section heading, keep the sitewide field out of the rail and text columns (`data-field-skip`), and end with "Keep reading": three cards from the reading queue instead of the old previous/next pair. Callouts, notices, standfirsts and sign-offs have their own styles. The Q2 2026 report gained two X post links on their own lines (the Kraken listing and the OKX and Bitpanda migration), which render as embeds. Post bodies no longer open with the cover image, and six no longer open with a line that repeats their description, since the header shows both. "The Augur Moon Fork Is Complete" had a description cut off with "..."; it now ends as the post's first line did ("…on Ethereum mainnet.").
- **Compound heading emoji**: [emoji.mjs](../../src/markdown/emoji.mjs) now strips a leading emoji joined with zero-width joiners (👨‍💻) whole. Before, "‍💻" stayed in one heading of "Micah's Augur Fork", and its anchor changed from `#-is-micah-still-developing-for-lituus` to `#is-micah-still-developing-for-lituus`.
- **Blog math**: the old site parsed `$…$` in posts as math, which showed "You don't spend $2 to steal $1." as code (Phase 2 post) and broke the dollar amounts, quotes and two links in "Augur Is Testing Its Past and Building Its Future". Both now render as written.
- **Icons**: the old React (`@phosphor-icons/react`) icons before every post heading are dropped, with the emoji they replaced.
- **Navigation**: the old React post bar and fork-guide drawer are static links ("Keep reading" on posts, `PagerNav` and `SectionRail` on the fork guides), and the old site's `migration-cta.tsx` was unused and is not ported. The whitepaper's React actions menu is three links (Markdown, PDF, source); "Copy page", "Open in ChatGPT" and "Open in Claude" are dropped. The Lituus whitepaper's disabled "View web" button is the note "Web edition not published yet."
- **Legal pages**: wording unchanged; the closing community links read Discord, Telegram and GitHub instead of capitals. [privacy.astro](../../src/pages/privacy.astro) carries a comment that its text awaits a legal rewrite.
- **Not carried over**: each whitepaper section's `<link rel="alternate">` to its Markdown, which [PageLayout.astro](../../src/layouts/PageLayout.astro) adds only for files in `public/`. The post pages' Open Graph image (their cover) is back through PageLayout's `image` prop ([hosting](hosting.md#page-metadata)).
